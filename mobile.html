About Us
Who We Are

What We Build

We design and hand-build solid wood tote rack systems sized exactly to your garage and your needs. Choose 1–6 columns wide and 2–6 rows high — every unit is cut, glued, and assembled to order. Add a plywood top, stain, heavy-duty casters, or a full set of 27-gallon storage totes, and we'll build it all in.

Our Commitment

We believe garage storage should be tough, good-looking, and built to last. Every frame is glued solid wood — no particle board, no wobble. Designed for real Canadian garages: they handle the cold, the weight, and the chaos of every season. Proudly built locally, delivering throughout the Burlington and Hamilton area.
