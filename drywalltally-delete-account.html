Privacy Policy

Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote (com.drywalltally)
Effective date: June 10, 2026

Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote ("the app") is a takeoff, estimating, and reporting tool for drywall contractors. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the app collects only what's needed to provide optional cloud backup, shows no ads, includes no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information (optional). You may create an account using an email address and password, or by signing in with Google. If you do, we receive your email address (and, with Google Sign-In, the basic profile name associated with your Google account). Creating an account is optional — the app is fully usable offline without one.
  • App content. Data you enter while using the app: job names, areas, material counts, price lists, custom items, your business information (company name, address, phone, email, HST number), and customer or site details you choose to record (such as a builder's name, site address, or contact information). This content exists to generate your reports and is under your control.

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • To provide the app's core features: tallying materials, calculating costs, and generating Purchase Order, Job Report, and Labor Payout PDFs.
  • If you sign in: to back up your app content to the cloud so it can be restored on the same or another device.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

  • On your device. All app content is stored locally on your device. Generated PDF reports are saved in the app's private storage and leave your device only when you choose to share them.
  • In the cloud (only if you sign in). Backups and account authentication are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's infrastructure, which may be located outside Canada (including the United States). Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services, solely to provide the features described above:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (account sign-in)
  • Google Cloud Firestore (cloud backup)
  • Google Play services (Google Sign-In)

No advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or social media trackers are included in the app.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backups are retained while your account exists so your data can be restored.
  • Deleting your data and account. You can request deletion of your cloud backup and account at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete the associated cloud data and authentication record within 30 days of a verified request. An in-app deletion option is also provided/being provided in the app's settings.

6. Your Information About Others

Job records may include business contact details of third parties (for example, a builder's site contact). You are responsible for ensuring you are permitted to record that information. It is stored and protected the same way as the rest of your app content and is never used by us for any purpose.

7. Security

Cloud data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with access restricted to your authenticated account. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we rely on industry-standard protections.

8. Children

The app is a professional contractor tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
