Delete Your Account & Data

Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote (com.drywalltally)
Developer: Benny Pacheco

You can permanently delete your Drywall Tally account and the cloud data associated with it at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — In the App (immediate)

  1. Open Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the gear icon (top right) to open the More menu.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm.
What gets deleted: your sign-in account (email or Google) and your entire cloud backup — jobs, areas, counts, price lists, custom items, and business information stored on our servers.

What is not deleted: data stored locally on your own device remains there until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app. PDF reports you previously shared or saved elsewhere are under your control.

For security, recent sign-in is required to delete an account. If the app asks you to sign in again, do so and repeat the steps — the deletion will complete.

Option 2 — By Email Request

Email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my Drywall Tally account". We will verify the request and delete your account and all associated cloud data within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

Data Retention

Once deleted, account and cloud data are not recoverable. We do not retain copies after deletion, except as required by law. For full details, see the Drywall Tally Privacy Policy.
