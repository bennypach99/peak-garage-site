Delete Your Account & Data

Developer: Benny Pacheco  ·  App package: com.drywalltally

You can delete your Drywall Tally account and all cloud data at any time, using either method below. No reason required, no hoops.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (instant)

Step 1 Open Drywall Tally and go to the More tab.
Step 2 Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm. If asked to sign in again, do so and repeat — recent sign-in is a Google security requirement.
Step 3 Done. Your account and all cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email benny.pach99@gmail.com with the subject "Drywall Tally account deletion", sent from the email address you used to sign in (so the request can be verified). Deletion is completed within 30 days and you'll receive a confirmation reply.

What gets deleted

DataResult
Your account (sign-in link, email, name)Deleted
All cloud data — jobs, areas, tallies, prices, reportsDeleted
Data stored on your deviceRemoved when you uninstall the app
Purchase records held by Google PlayRetained by Google under Google's privacy policy — not controlled by the developer

No data is retained by the developer after deletion.

Subscribed to Pro? Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel first in the Play Store → Subscriptions page, then delete your account.
