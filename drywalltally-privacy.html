Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote

Privacy Policy

Effective June 10, 2026  ·  App package: com.drywalltally

Drywall Tally: Takeoff & Quote ("the app") is developed and operated by Benny Pacheco, Peak Garage Solutions, Burlington, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions any time: benny.pach99@gmail.com.

What the app collects

DataWhenWhy
Email address and name Only if you sign in (optional) — with Google or an email & password To create your account and identify your cloud backup
Your takeoff data — jobs, areas, sheet counts, prices, and reports you create Only if you sign in and enable cloud sync To back up your work and restore it on reinstall or a new device

That's the whole list. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking libraries. If you never sign in, all of your data stays on your device and nothing is collected at all.

How your data is stored and protected

Cloud data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is encrypted in transit using TLS. Your data is stored under your own account and is not accessible to other users of the app.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing. The only parties that process data on the app's behalf are:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and cloud storage, as described above
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are handled entirely by Google and are never visible to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

In the app: More → Delete Account (immediate), or see the full instructions at peakgaragesolutions.ca/drywalltally-delete-account.html

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is a professional contracting tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
